- Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Governs: `path/to/code`, `path/to/other`

## Context

Why this question came up. What we tried or considered. Keep the facts
that made the choice non-obvious; drop the ones that didn't.

## Decision

What we do. Present tense, one or two paragraphs.

## Consequences

What this buys, what it costs, what it rules out. Be as honest here as the
README's "What it's not" section is.
